## Part 2 — Metric catalogue

Every indicator the platform computes, with its definition and its default red/amber/green thresholds. Thresholds are starting points for a tertiary centre and are overridden per facility through `PUT /api/v1/admin/benchmarks`, which is why the API always returns the target alongside the value.

Sources: **CBAHI** Saudi Central Board for Accreditation of Healthcare Institutions · **JCI** Joint Commission International · **MAGNET** ANCC Magnet Recognition / NDNQI · **HSTP** Saudi Health Sector Transformation Programme · **INTERNAL** operational convention with no external standard.

## Part 3 — Conventions that affect every number

**Patient days are occupancy-weighted, not midnight census.** A stay from 22:00 Monday to 06:00 Wednesday contributes 0.083 days to Monday, 1.0 to Tuesday and 0.25 to Wednesday. Midnight counting makes short-stay and observation activity disappear.

**Timing indicators report medians.** ED and journey intervals have long right tails; one 30-hour boarder moves a mean far more than it moves the typical patient's experience. The 90th percentile is carried in each tile's `context` so the tail stays visible.

**Harm rates count hospital-acquired events only.** Events flagged `present_on_admission` are excluded from the numerator. Mixing the two is the most common reason an infection rate fails an accreditation survey.

**Emergency treatment spaces are not inpatient beds.** They are excluded from the occupancy denominator and reported separately by the emergency module. Nursing indicators do include the ED, because emergency nurses care for real patients whose hours need a real denominator.

**Rates return `null`, never zero, when the denominator is empty.** A closed unit reports "no data", not 0%.

**Readmission looks forward past the reporting window.** The denominator is index discharges inside the period; the numerator searches the following 30 days, so the repository deliberately loads a wider slice than the window itself.
